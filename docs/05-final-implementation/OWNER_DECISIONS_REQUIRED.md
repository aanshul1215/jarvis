# Owner decisions before implementation and paper orders

These are inputs to request and record; no values are assumed here. Record approvals in a versioned local policy document without credentials or private account numbers.

- Tradable equity universe, liquidity filter, BTC/ETH observation versus paper-trading eligibility, and strategy horizons.
- Paper capital matching intended scale; maximum order, position, portfolio, crypto exposure and loss; quote age/spread limits; kill and re-arm authority.
- Source permissions, local data retention, `JARVIS_HOME` location, encrypted backup destination and restore objective.
- Paper account owner, technical reviewer, independent quant/risk reviewer, and strategy-promotion signoff.
- First model and strategy baseline, trial budget, holdout period and acceptance metrics before results are inspected.
- Laptop uptime tolerance and handling of positions while the machine sleeps; no claim of 24/7 protection from laptop software.

Live trading remains OFF until a separate A5 readiness review and explicit owner approval. Unanswered inputs mean mock/shadow or FLAT as appropriate.
