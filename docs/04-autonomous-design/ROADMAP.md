# Autonomous JARVIS delivery roadmap

**Status:** proposed sequence, not a schedule or a claim of trading performance. Each milestone ends with an observable result. Paper and live modes share the same decision and risk code; only the broker destination and owner-approved permission differ. Keep research and negative results in the ledger.

| Phase | Work | Exit test |
|---|---|---|
| **A0 — Secure and verify** | Revoke exposed legacy/search keys; check account access, Texas spot BTC/ETH eligibility, supported order types, fees, market-data entitlements, news/social licences, Windows sleep/restart behaviour; obtain the owner's universe and risk-envelope inputs. | Probe register marks each dependency pass, fail, or an explicit fallback. Live mode remains impossible without a signed risk envelope. |
| **A1 — Replayable data and ledger** | Implement asset/entity IDs, `AssetEvent`, point-in-time `FeatureSnapshot`, SQLite audit/queue, Parquet bars, source adapters, quality checks, local backup/restore. Import legacy TSLA material only as teaching and test fixtures, never as a trusted live model. | Replay of a chosen date reproduces identical inputs. Future-data and old TSLA label-leak fixtures are blocked; a backup restores cleanly. |
| **A2 — Information Intelligence and scan** | Build the bounded market scanner; independent text queue; deduplication, entity mapping, local summaries, SEC/news/social event features; asset-specific freshness rules. Keep unsupported social sources inactive but retain the adapter contract. | Broad scan runs within the 8 GB budget, shortlist is bounded, duplicate items are processed once, text backlog never delays a market decision, and an expired required feature produces FLAT. |
| **A3 — Strategies and automatic paper trading** | Define frozen strategy specs and baselines for equity and BTC/ETH lanes; selector, risk gate, intent-first broker adapter, position monitor and exact reason-code reports. Start with simple, transparent baselines; evaluate old XGBoost/FinBERT/SHAP ideas only as challengers. | Full OBSERVE→LEARN paper replay and broker run completes without manual orders. Mock-broker restart, duplicate, partial-fill, reject, foreign-order, and outage drills pass. Every order and no-trade has a trace. |
| **A4 — Controlled learning** | Add trial registry, walk-forward cost-aware harness, shadow challenger, component calibration, attribution, promotion and rollback machinery. Freeze evaluation rules before examining each candidate result. | A planted look-ahead oracle fails, a random strategy is not promoted, a versioned challenger can be shadowed and rolled back, and an inconclusive low-sample result cannot reach live trading. |
| **A5 — Owner-enabled live pilot** | Owner reviews the operating envelope and switches on the already-tested live adapter; enable only validated strategy families, assets and pilot exposure. Compare live spread, fees, slippage, rejected orders and downtime with paper assumptions. | No unexplained broker state; every fill and fee reconciled; kill/SAFE actions and restart drills demonstrated on the live account; owner can disable live trading immediately. Expansion requires a separate recorded decision. |

## Required verification scenarios

- **Data and text:** duplicate posts, recycled article URLs, wrong ticker/entity, out-of-order and revised filings, corporate actions, source outage, future timestamp, social manipulation, prompt-like article text, late summarizer result, and an unexpectedly large text backlog.
- **Decision and statistics:** no-data/FLAT, conflicting signals, feature expiry, price-only baseline, one-bar lag, costs doubled, trade frequency and selection bias, calibration failure, multiple trials, and performance after taxes where applicable. A summary or SHAP explanation alone must never produce an order.
- **Orders and account:** stale or wide quotes, insufficient buying power, rejected order, partial fill, timeout before and after submission, duplicate ID, cancel/replace, unknown broker order, deposit, withdrawal, dividend, crypto fee posted later, market close, and restart after a laptop sleep. An old candidate expires rather than firing on recovery.
- **Controls:** loss/exposure cap, crypto eligibility change, data-trust failure, risk-limit loosening, new strategy family, large-order approval, temporary degraded-data request, backup restore, missing backup, and immediate owner kill.

## What constitutes useful progress

Technical gates prove that JARVIS can observe, decide, trade and recover correctly. A separate evidence gate asks whether each strategy or text feature improves a declared baseline **after costs**. Paper fills are not evidence of a profitable live edge: Alpaca says paper trading does not model market impact, latency slippage, or queue position. [Paper-trading specification](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/v1.4.2/docs/paper-trading). If the evidence is weak or sample size is inadequate, the system remains in paper/shadow or uses FLAT; milestone completion never implies a guaranteed return.
