# Critique: broker-execution-mechanics (2026-10-01)

Lens: does every order, stop and reconciliation step work on Alpaca as documented? Evidence: briefs 05, 21, 17 (plus 12, 23, 19, 06 where they bear on the same mechanics). All five proposals converge on the same shape (long/flat ETF trend book, run-to-completion jobs, intent-first idempotent orders, broker-resting stops, REST reconciliation). So most findings are cross-cutting; the differences are in how many mechanics each one specifies and how many it gets wrong.

Verdict: no proposal has a fatal flaw on this lens. Every proposal has the same set of specification gaps that would show up as stuck rebalances, false HALTs or unprotected positions during paper/micro-live, not as a direct money loss. D is the best on this lens, then B, then A and C, then E.

## Facts used (all from briefs)

- Fractional orders: market/limit/stop/stop-limit, TIF DAY only, no fractional shorting, $1 min notional; notional orders cannot be replaced (21, 05, 17). A fractional position cannot carry a GTC stop.
- Whole-share GTC stops auto-cancel after 90 days (21, 17, 23). Standalone stop = no bracket/OCO outside regular hours; extended hours limit-only.
- Crypto: market/limit/stop-limit, GTC/IOC only, no bracket/OCO/trailing, no margin/short; fees 15/25 bps on the credited asset, posted end of day (21, 05). Texas absent from a year-old, snippet-only 28-state list (21): treat crypto as probably unavailable until confirmed.
- Alpaca self-cross guard: opposite-side market orders always rejected 403; opposite limits when buy >= sell; bracket/OCO/trailing exempt (12, 21).
- Replace returns a NEW order id and success does not mean the old order is gone; no documented trade_updates replay after reconnect; duplicate client_order_id behaviour undocumented (21, 17).
- Paper does not model regulatory fees, dividends, price improvement, impact; random 10% partial fills; IEX only; unknown whether paper enforces the $2,000 floor / intraday margin / crypto state limits (05, 21, 17).
- Basic data: IEX only (~2.5% of volume), 30 WS symbols, 200 REST/min, SIP history older than 15 minutes (06, 05).
- All accounts margin; below $2,000 equity multiplier 1; IRA via Trading API exists (equities/options, 1x, no crypto, trustee Equity Trust, fee unknown, paper IRA not stated) (21, 19, 05).
- Customer agreement s.32 lets Alpaca keep legacy PDT per account; s.28 limits sub-$1 / repeated sub-cent notional orders; "Risks of Automated Trading" says algorithms initially run "on your own computer ... not a server" (21).

## Cross-cutting defects (apply to all five unless a proposal is named as the exception)

1. **Self-cross rule vs protective stops.** A section 6 ("no opposite-side order while one is working"), D F14 and section 2 step 12, E section 6 ("Self-cross: No opposite-side order while one is working") write a gate rule that a held whole-share position's own resting GTC sell stop violates. Any add-to-position or partial trim then hits the in-house gate (default-deny, so it deadlocks), and Alpaca's own guard rejects opposite-side market orders always (brief 12 line 157) and may treat a buy against a resting sell stop as a self-cross (untested; stop-vs-limit wording is not in the docs). B and C say "no self-cross" without the exemption. Fix: protective stops are an exempt order class; prefer OTO/bracket/OCO or trailing (exempt from Alpaca's guard) for protection, as brief 17 rec 8 says ("prefer broker-native bracket/OCO"). No proposal uses them.
2. **Exit sequencing with a resting stop.** A resting stop holds the shares, so a signal-flip sell or partial trim (A "partial adjustment inside the band", 20-25% no-trade bands everywhere) is rejected for insufficient available qty. The required sequence is cancel stop, wait for a terminal state (pending_cancel, order_cancel_rejected possible), sell, then re-arm on remaining qty. Between those steps there is an unprotected window and a cancel/fill race (brief 17 F20: double-sell, accidental short). Brief 23 line 116 lists quantity locking as untested. None of the five specify this sequence or the brief 17 rec 8 rule that a sell-to-close never exceeds broker-reported position. Fractional remainders compound it (the stop covers floor(qty) only).
3. **Broker-side leverage/short lock.** Only B sets `no_shorting` at the broker; D reads account flags and goes SAFE on surprises. A, C, E enforce leverage 1.0 and no shorting only in their own gate. Above $2,000 (most of the $1k-$10k range) Alpaca defaults to 2x/4x margin with 6.5% interest (docs figure; brief 05 verification). With the double-sell race in item 2, the broker-side lock (`max_margin_multiplier`, `no_shorting`, brief 21 rec 3) is the only layer independent of the gate.
4. **Kill switch cancels the protection.** D: "`jarvis kill` ... cancels all orders (`DELETE /v2/orders`)"; A, B, C, E: "cancel all, block new". Cancel-all removes every GTC stop and crypto stop-limit, leaving all positions unprotected in the incident that triggered the kill. The kill should cancel only non-protective orders, and the flatten path must use close-all with `cancel_orders=true` (otherwise shares held by stops block closing; brief 12 line 347, brief 17 F21). Market flatten is rejected outside regular hours for equities (17 F21), so the lifetime-stop "flatten to cash" in B, D, E needs a market-closed path (limit extended-hours for whole shares; fractional extended-hours behaviour is ambiguous in brief 21 verification).
5. **Reconciliation will false-alarm in live.** Every proposal reconciles positions and cash and sets SAFE/HALT on any mismatch (A step 14/section 3, B step 2, C step 2, D step 3, E step 2). Paper does not simulate dividends or regulatory fees (05, 17 F10), so G3 passes with zero breaks; the first live bond-ETF dividend, cent-rounded regulatory fee, crypto fee-on-credited-asset, split, or catastrophe-stop fill between runs is then an unexplained diff. No proposal ingests broker account activities/fills as expected ledger events or defines tolerances. The first micro-live month (G4) would HALT on normal events. Stop-outs are the worst case: the protection working looks like a reconciliation break (brief 23: "stop already filled while ledger says holding").
6. **Risk-off rotation throttled by entry controls.** A trend flip to T-bill ETF is a buy of up to 100% of equity in one symbol. A: per-order cap min(30%, $3,000) (T-bill exempt only from the per-symbol cap); B: $350 per order at $1,000, 30% per-asset cap, "same symbol and side within 10 minutes blocked"; C: duplicate guard on "same symbol/side/size within 10 minutes"; D: min(30%, $4,000), 30% per ETF; E: min(30%, $3,000), same 10-minute rule. The de-risking event, the whole point of the strategy, then takes several runs or deadlocks. Risk-reducing and parking orders need their own limit class; also nobody sequences sells before buys or reads broker `buying_power` (brief 05 rec 4). Whether sale proceeds are reusable the same day in a 1x sub-$2,000 account is undocumented (brief 21 open items).
7. **IEX-only quote as the gate's reference.** "Quote no older than 60 s" with a 0.5% collar (B, C, E), 1% (A), 1.5% (D) is checked against Basic-plan quotes that are one venue's book, not NBBO (06). Thin ETFs and T-bill ETFs often have no fresh IEX quote, so a 60-second freshness rule can block orders on quiet names. D alone notes the collar is wider because quotes are IEX-only (D F3). Nobody plans a probe of IEX quote staleness for the chosen ETFs.
8. **Unfilled and partial order policy absent.** All say "poll to terminal". None define what happens to a DAY marketable limit that expires unfilled (reprice via cancel-and-resubmit with attempt+1 after confirming terminal state; never replace a notional order), partial fills (stop qty follows filled qty, remainder policy; brief 17 rec 8), or dust below minimum order (agreement s.28). Paper's random 10% partials will exercise this immediately.
9. **Crypto path.** Texas is absent from the (year-old, snippet-only) Alpaca state list (21). Every proposal treats crypto as conditional, correctly. But: B's fallback "Alpaca spot on a Linux host" fails if Texas is excluded; the spot-ETF alternative is unresearched (00b) and would be fractional/DAY-only with whole-share stops only. Where crypto is allowed: stop sized to the filled quantity, but fees are taken from the credited asset and posted end of day (21), so position qty can differ from the fill; whether crypto GTC stop-limit survives 90 days, accepts IOC, or locks quantity is untested (23 line 116, 21 uncertainty 4). The 3 to 6% gap-loss rule (A: 10% x 30%; C: 20% x 30% = 6%) assumes a stop-limit fills.
10. **VM move vs Alpaca automation disclosure.** All five move to a GCP e2-micro/VPS before crypto or after missed runs. Brief 21 reports the "Risks of Automated Trading" disclosure saying algorithms initially run on the customer's own computer, not a server (risk language, medium). None asks Alpaca or flags it. Treat the move as conditional on written confirmation.
11. **IRA is treated as a drop-in.** A, B, C, D, E each say "move sleeve A to the IRA if fee acceptable". Mechanics missing: a second account (separate keys, ledger partition, reconciliation, kill switch, G4 micro-live), no paper IRA stated (21), contribution cap $7,500/yr (19) so a $10,000 book cannot be funded in one year without rollover/ACATS, settlement/GFV behaviour unknown (21 uncertainty 8), crypto sleeve stays in a separate taxable account. Moving a validated strategy to a new account type must restart G3/G4 for that account; none says so.
12. **Probes at P0.** Brief 21 lists nine questions only a real account can answer. A P0 lists duplicate client_order_id and websocket replay spikes plus Texas. C P0 lists news, duplicate id, Texas, IRA fees. B P0 lists Texas, IRA, "API behaviour". D P0 lists Texas, IRA fee, news access, agreement; duplicate id only as a GF drill. E P0 has no broker probes at all. None probes: stop+buy self-cross, quantity locking by a resting stop, cancel-vs-stop-trigger race, GTC across splits, whether paper enforces $2,000/1x/crypto-state rules, IEX quote staleness, same-day reuse of sale proceeds.
13. **Small items.** PDT/intraday-margin rejection handlers (21 rec 3; agreement s.32): present in C and D (F12), absent in A, B, E. Paper balance reset to real capital (17 rec 7): present in A, B, E (G3/G4), absent in C and D. Cash buffer for cent-rounded fees: only E (2%); others invest 100% via a T-bill ETF and risk a small negative cash that borrows at 6.5%. News `first_seen_at` is only as fine as the polling cadence (twice-daily jobs), so it is not a true first-seen time (21 recommends archiving own received_at).

## Per-proposal

### A-vision-faithful (6.5)
- Strengths: P0 spikes on duplicate `client_order_id` and websocket replay (best probe list after C); intent-before-broker-call; lookup before resubmit; GTC stops re-armed each run; crypto gap rule capped at 3% of equity with a 10% launch sleeve; paper balance = real capital; G3 drills; G4 cost <= 1.5x model; explicit that fractional slots are unprotected and why (resolves gap item 15); crypto run is conditional and moves to a VM first.
- Flaws: section 6 "no opposite-side order while one is working" vs resting stops (defect 1); "partial adjustment inside the band" with a stop holding shares (2); kill = "cancel all, block new" (4); no PDT handler (13); no broker-side `no_shorting` (3); reconciliation false alarms (5); per-order cap blocks risk-off T-bill buy (6); "REST reconcile after reconnect" is vestigial (the design has no stream), harmless. Section 8 schedules a 00:05 UTC crypto run on the laptop but defers the VM only to "before the first live crypto position", which is fine.
- Fatal: none.

### B-evidence-minimal (7.0)
- Strengths: the only proposal that sets `no_shorting` at the broker and in the gate; whole-share-only book so every overnight position can carry a stop (consistent with brief 23 rec 5); lookup-by-ID before resubmit; approvals never block risk-reducing orders; paper balance = real capital; clear micro-live cost gate; Healthchecks plus stop re-arm after polling.
- Flaws: whole shares at $1,000 with 4 sleeves means one share price can be a large fraction of a ~$250 slot; "rounding under 10% of each target weight" forces share prices of about $25 or less per slot (arithmetic), which the proposal never shows is satisfiable with liquid ETFs; T-bill residual conflicts with "max weight 30%" and the $350 per-order cap (6); 3x ATR(20) "catastrophe stop" is a tight stop (about a 1.5% to 3% move on low-vol ETFs), contradicting brief 17 F3 and rec 2 (wide catastrophe stop only) and likely to cause stop-outs and wash sales on a monthly strategy; kill = cancel all (4); no PDT handler (13); only three drills; strategy B fallback "Alpaca spot on a Linux host" ignores Texas and the own-computer disclosure (9, 10); execution at 10:15 ET relies on IEX quote 60 s freshness with a tight 0.5% collar (7).
- Fatal: none.

### C-agents-as-quant-team (6.5)
- Strengths: P0 probes (news, duplicate id, Texas, IRA fees); PDT/intraday-margin handlers; Incident Diagnostician with a redacted broker snapshot (useful for reconciliation triage); stops sized from state; separate Windows user for the live key; states plainly that at $1,000 most of the book will be fractional and unprotected.
- Flaws: accepts unprotected fractional lots as the default at launch, so for most of the range the stop machinery barely applies (the system-death mitigation reduces to the alert and the owner), and brief 17 rec 10 (smaller limits for unprotectable positions) is not adopted; duplicate guard "same symbol/side/size within 10 minutes" blocks equal-sized chunks of a split rebalance (6); "cash >= 0 after open orders" is good but fees posted later are not handled (5, 13); G3 lacks paper balance = real capital and gives only three drills; crypto 20% sleeve gap = 6% of equity rests on a stop-limit filling (9); kill = cancel all (4).
- Fatal: none.

### D-failure-first (7.5)
- Strengths: the only proposal with a constraints adapter that reads account status, buying power, shorting/crypto/margin flags and fee tier every run and goes SAFE on anything unexpected; reconciles unknown orders/positions (manual trade triggers SAFE); a "Protection check" step (13) plus a separate "stops verified" Healthchecks check; GF drill list covers kill mid-order, duplicate id, partial fill, replace race, 5xx, foreign manual order, clock skew, restore, and kill switch (the most complete against brief 17 F19/F20 and 23); typed never-retried handlers for self-cross, PDT and margin rejections (matches 12 line 159); no blind replace retry; marketable limits only, 09:45-15:30 ET; collar widened because quotes are IEX-only; crypto gated behind S-A reaching G4 and a host move; whole-share GTC stops re-armed each run.
- Flaws: F14 and step 12 forbid opposite-side orders while one is working, which conflicts with its own resting stops (1); `jarvis kill` = `DELETE /v2/orders` removes all stops (4); step 3 reconciliation will flag dividends/fees/stop fills (5); whole-share rule needs share prices that keep weights "within 5 points" at $1,000, unproven (B-style); 3x ATR stop is tight (brief 17 F3); 1.5% ETF collar is loose against brief 17 F13's 0.07% to 0.46% real retail round trip; G3 lacks paper balance reset; P0 does not probe the duplicate id (drill only); still moves to a VM (10).
- Fatal: none.

### E-staged-platform (5.5)
- Strengths: correct statement of the fractional consequence ("most legs at this size are fractional, hence DAY-only, so no stop can rest"); 2% cash buffer and $20 minimum order (covers cent-rounded fees and dust; agreement s.28); paper balance = real capital in G3; B-crypto's Donchian exit as a GTC stop-limit re-armed each run; evening/morning job split.
- Flaws: launch S1 has no resting protection at all (Strategy A "exits on the monthly SMA signal with no intra-month stop"), with sleeves 20% each and no smaller limits for the unprotectable (brief 17 rec 10, brief 23 rec 5); the owner accepts it, but system death with an open drawdown is only an alert; P0 has no broker probes (defect 12), so duplicate-id, replay and Texas answers arrive late; no PDT/intraday-margin handler (13); no per-symbol cap; "Self-cross: No opposite-side order while one is working" conflicts with later whole-share stops (1); "kill cancels everything" (4); crypto S3 relies on unconfirmed Texas and a VM (9, 10); IRA S4a drop-in (11).
- Fatal: none.

## Ideas worth grafting

- B: `no_shorting` and `max_margin_multiplier=1` set in the broker account config, in addition to the gate.
- D: broker-constraints adapter at the top of every run; reconcile treats unknown orders/positions/manual trades as SAFE; protection-check step plus a separate "stops verified" heartbeat; the GF drill list (add stop-fill-between-runs and cancel-vs-stop race); typed, never-retried handlers for 403 self-cross, PDT and margin rejections; trading window 09:45-15:30 ET with marketable limits only; collar wider because quotes are IEX-only.
- C: PDT/intraday-margin rejection handlers; Incident Diagnostician reading a redacted broker snapshot.
- A: P0 spikes on duplicate client_order_id and trade_updates replay before any code depends on them; whole-share slots get stops, fractional explicitly unprotected; crypto gap rule (30% gap must cost <= 3% of equity).
- E: 2% cash buffer, $20 minimum order, paper balance = real capital.
- D/B/A: paper balance reset to real capital in G3 (brief 17 rec 7).

## Missed by all five

1. A complete "stop lifecycle" spec: arm after fill sized to filled qty, cancel-confirm-sell on exit, resize after trims/adds, re-arm before 90-day expiry via cancel + new client id, split/halt handling, and an exemption from the in-house self-cross rule; preferably OTO/OCO (exempt from Alpaca's guard) tested in paper.
2. Ingestion of broker account activities (fills, dividends, fees, splits) into the ledger with reconciliation tolerances; stop-out fills treated as expected events with a defined re-entry rule (and wash-sale flag).
3. A kill switch that preserves protective orders, plus a market-closed flatten path.
4. Sell-first sequencing, use of broker `buying_power`, and a separate limit class for risk-reducing/parking orders so risk-off rotation is not throttled by entry caps.
5. A P0 probe list covering every open item in brief 21 section "Open uncertainties" plus IEX quote staleness for the chosen ETFs and whether paper enforces the $2,000/1x/crypto-state rules; and a rule that paper results never relax a live check.
6. Written Alpaca confirmation (support) on running the bot on a VM, crypto availability in Texas, and IRA trustee fees, before any of the three is relied on; and a re-run of G3/G4 for any new account (IRA) or host.
7. Multi-account design (IRA + taxable, crypto cannot go in the IRA) for ledger, reconciliation, per-account kill switch and keys.
